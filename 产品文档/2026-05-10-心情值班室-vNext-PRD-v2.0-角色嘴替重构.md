## 0. Change Summary

This PRD updates the current 心情值班室 direction after DavidC's product review. The main shift is:

- From **“动物皮肤 + 场景文案”** to **“用户认同的人格化嘴替”**.
- From **“简单嘴替工具”** to **“让用户觉得这像我/像我会说的话，但由它替我承担表达成本”**.
- From **“结果页随便换角色”** to **“每个场景固定 3 个合适嘴替，用户可在同一个表达场景里换另一个嘴替看看”**.
- From **“调语气 slider”** to **“换个说法”**：不量化用户对结果的评价，不让用户调强弱维度。
- From **“角色标签说明”** to **“角色名称 + 角色声线 + quotes，让用户自行带入”**.

This PRD does **not** modify the current online v1.2 immediately. It defines vNext direction and handoff boundaries.

## 1. Executive Summary

We are building a lightweight H5 for in-school students who want to share or 保存 an emotional state without personally bearing the full social cost of saying it. Users select a campus-life state, choose a mouthpiece persona that feels like “how I would handle this”, generate a shareable result image, and optionally reword or switch to one of two alternate mouthpieces from the same scene pool.

The product should make users feel: **“这很像我”** and **“这句话我不想自己说，但它替我说了”**. The role is not a decoration; it is the expression strategy.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

### 2.1 Who has this problem?

Primary TA: in-school college students, especially high-social-density students who regularly face dorm/class/group-chat/club/DDL interactions and need to manage how they present emotional states.

Working profile:
- Age: mainly 18–22.
- Context: dorm, class, group work, friend circles, school organizations, DDL, social invitations, messages.
- Gender: likely female-skew in early adoption, but persona and visual system must avoid stereotypically “female” framing.
- Behavior: uses shareable images, memes, quotes, QQ/朋友圈/小红书-like visual language to express mood indirectly.

### 2.2 What is the problem?

The user often has a state they want to express, but direct expression is socially costly:

- Saying too much feels vulnerable.
- Saying nothing may be misunderstood.
- Replying may invite more interaction.
- Complaining directly may seem aggressive.
- Explaining seriously may be exhausting or embarrassing.

The user needs a low-risk expression carrier that feels personally resonant but slightly separated from the self.

### 2.3 Why is it painful?

Users are not simply unable to speak. They are managing:

- self-image: “我不想显得很严重/很脆弱/很难相处”;
- audience ambiguity: classmates, friends, acquaintances, roommates may all see it;
- social aftercare: a post may invite questions or replies;
- tone risk: too soft feels exposing, too sharp feels conflictual, too abstract may be unreadable.

### 2.4 Product opportunity

A mouthpiece persona can act as a buffer layer:

- It carries the sentence so the user does not have to fully own it.
- It makes the expression feel like “a style/persona”, not a confession.
- It turns emotion into a shareable object.
- It lets users recognize themselves without being explicitly diagnosed.

## 3. Product Principles

### P1. Recognition before explanation

The product should help users feel “这像我” before explaining what the product does.

### P2. Mouthpiece is a persona, not a skin

Each role must have a distinct internal motive, expression style, and visible voice. A role is not just a visual avatar.

### P3. Show, don't tell

Do not tell users “this role fits you because...”. Show role personality via image, name, short style phrase, and quotes. Let users infer fit.

### P4. Avoid triggering defensiveness

External descriptions must avoid making users feel judged, exposed, weak, lazy, socially incapable, or aggressive. Internal definitions can be sharper; front-stage copy must be tactful.

### P5. No quantifiable emotion controls

Do not use tone slider, intensity scale, attack/funny/softness meters, or any control that asks users to score their emotional state.

### P6. ≤ 3 steps to result

The primary generation flow should remain simple. No platform selection, no long personality quiz, no full 5-role matrix upfront.

## 4. Target User & Jobs-to-be-Done

### 4.1 Primary user

A socially-aware in-school student who wants to express a momentary emotional/social state while controlling how much responsibility, vulnerability, or conflict the expression carries.

### 4.2 Core JTBD

When I am in a campus/social situation where I have a feeling but do not want to say it directly, I want to pick a mouthpiece that sounds like how I would handle it, so I can generate something shareable/保存able that feels like me without forcing me to explain myself.

### 4.3 Usage jobs

- Signal a state without inviting too much questioning.
- Make a private mood feel light enough to share.
- Turn a frustrating/awkward situation into a meme-like expression.
- Say “no / later / not okay” without sounding harsh.
- Save a result as a self-recognition card even if not posted.

## 5. P0 User Flow

### 5.1 Main flow

```text
Step 1: 选择状态/场景
Step 2: 选择嘴替/表达人格
Step 3: 生成结果页
```

### 5.2 Result page actions

```text
结果图
→ 当前嘴替角色卡
→ 换个说法
→ 换个嘴替帮你说
→ 保存 / 复制配文 / 重选状态
```

### 5.3 Why not platform selection?

Platform is temporarily removed from the P0 decision tree. Users may implicitly pick a role based on where they intend to share, but the product should not ask them to choose “朋友圈/QQ/小红书版” as an input variable.

### 5.4 Why not tone slider?

Tone sliders make the result feel quantifiable and invite users to judge whether they are “too emotional / too aggressive / not funny enough”. This contradicts the product goal of low-friction identification.

## 6. Role System

P0 contains five mouthpiece roles. These are internal role types; final role names are not locked.

### A. 嘴硬漏风型

Internal motive: has a state but does not want to admit or explain it.

User-facing feeling:嘴上没事，细节全在出卖。

Expression mode:
- mouth-hard;
- restrained;
- slightly leaking;
- “别问/没事/装得还行”.

Avoid:
- vulnerability labels;
- comfort-seeking;
- “I am weak/sad” framing.

### B. 离线挡门型

Internal motive: replying or interacting itself is the burden.

User-facing feeling:不是冷，是对话入口暂时关了。

Expression mode:
- offline;
- do-not-disturb;
- boundary;
- low response.

Avoid:
- arrogance;
- cold violence;
- “懒得理你”.

### C. 加载未启动型

Internal motive: knows what should be done, but cannot initiate.

User-facing feeling:任务在那，人也在这，中间没连上。

Expression mode:
- loading;
- stuck but aware;
- self-mocking;
- boot failure.

Avoid:
- irresponsible;
- blame-shifting;
- heavy failure.

### D. 抽象发疯型

Internal motive: serious explanation is exhausting; turning the state into a weird readable object reduces burden.

User-facing feeling:离谱但成立。

Expression mode:
- abstract;
- meme-like;
- weird but understandable;
- “精神跑偏但还在维持”.

Avoid:
- random nonsense;
- mental illness stigma;
- overly normal copy.

### E. 轻刺反骨型

Internal motive: dissatisfied, offended, or pushed around, but does not want direct conflict.

User-facing feeling:有点不服，但不掀桌。

Expression mode:
- controlled edge;
- lightly rebellious;
- sharp but not mean;
- “我配合，不代表我认同”.

Avoid:
- personal attacks;
- serious conflict escalation;
- exposing private disputes.

## 7. P0 Scene Scope & Role Pools

P0 does not implement all `6 × 5 = 30` combinations. Each scene has a fixed 3-role pool for relevance and clarity.

| Scene | External scene direction | Role pool | Default role | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 想把话轻轻带过 | A / B / D | A | Internal motive: 被问“你怎么了”但不想解释. Front-stage wording must stay neutral. |
| S2 | 消息看见了，但现在接不住 | B / A / E | B | Not “I ignore you”; conversation entry is temporarily closed. |
| S3 | 任务在那，但人还没启动 | C / D / E | C | DDL/作业/论文/被催 but stuck with self-awareness. |
| S4 | 校园日常突然离谱 | D / E / C | D | D's main field; abstract must be visible. |
| S5 | 现在不想被拉进互动 | B / A / E | B | Dorm/social invitation/quiet boundary; avoid hostility. |
| S6 | 这事离谱，但不想亲自开怼 | E / C / D | E | Group work/being pushed/being arranged; sharp but controlled. |

Total effective combinations: 18.

## 8. Result Page Requirements

### 8.1 Result page blocks

1. **Result image**  
   Generated share image based on scene + current role.

2. **Current mouthpiece role card**  
   Shows role image, role name, style phrase, personality line, and one quote. It does not explain fit.

3. **换个说法**  
   Same scene, same role, same safety boundary; only changes expression variant.

4. **换个嘴替帮你说**  
   Shows the other two roles from the fixed scene role pool. Each card shows role image, role name, style phrase, and optionally a quote preview.

5. **Save/copy/reselect actions**  
   Preserve simple output behavior.

### 8.2 Current role card data

```ts
CurrentRoleCard = {
  roleName,
  roleImage,
  stylePhrase,
  personalityLine,
  quote
}
```

### 8.3 Alternate role card data

```ts
AlternateRoleCard = {
  roleName,
  roleImage,
  stylePhrase,
  quotePreview
}
```

### 8.4 Quote data

```ts
RoleQuote = {
  roleId,
  quoteId,
  text,
  usage: 'role_card' | 'alternate_card' | 'result_support',
  intensity: 'safe' | 'strong',
  sceneFit?: string[]
}
```

P0 quote bank target:
- 7 quotes per role;
- 5 roles;
- about 35 quotes total.

### 8.5 Interaction state machine

```ts
state = {
  sceneId,
  currentRoleId,
  rolePool: [roleA, roleB, roleC],
  currentVariantId
}
```

Initialization:
- Load scene's fixed role pool.
- Set `currentRoleId` to scene default.
- Render default result.
- Block4 shows the other two roles in the same pool.

Click `换个说法`:
- `sceneId` unchanged.
- `currentRoleId` unchanged.
- `rolePool` unchanged.
- Only `currentVariantId` changes.

Click alternate mouthpiece:
- `sceneId` unchanged.
- `rolePool` unchanged.
- `currentRoleId` changes.
- `currentVariantId` resets to the clicked role's default variant for this scene.
- Block4 continues to show the other two roles from the same fixed pool.

## 9. Content Requirements

### 9.1 All role-related copy must carry role voice

This applies to:
- role names;
- style phrases;
- personality lines;
- quotes;
- result line variants;
- alternate CTA cards;
- any short text attached to a role.

### 9.2 Do not write neutral product labels as role content

Bad direction:
- “适合不想解释的你”
- “它会帮你委婉表达”
- “该角色用于处理社交边界”

Better direction:
- Let the role speak or behave.
- Let users infer.

### 9.3 Final naming is not locked

Role names, page labels, component names, and final lines should be handled by copy specialist after this PRD is accepted.

---

## 10. Visual Requirements

### 10.1 Role visuals must start from voice

Do not first draw animals and then paste personality onto them. Start from the role's internal motive and expression posture.

### 10.2 Personality differentiation

Each role should be recognizable by:
- posture;
- expression;
- silhouette;
- visual rhythm;
- relationship to the result card.

### 10.3 No gender-stereotype shortcut

Even if the early TA is female-skew, visuals and role personality should not default to pink/soft/cute/feminized tropes.

### 10.4 Quality line

All world assets must share the same production quality. Placeholder-level, CSS/emoji-generated, low-quality pasted assets are not allowed in final.

## 11. Metrics & Success Criteria

### 11.1 Product quality criteria

A user should be able to understand:
- what state they selected;
- which mouthpiece is speaking;
- what personality the mouthpiece has;
- why the result feels like a self-expression object without being explicitly told.

### 11.2 Behavioral metrics for later test

Potential metrics after implementation:
- result generation completion rate;
- role switch rate on result page;
- `换个说法` usage rate;
- save/share/copy rate;
- repeat generation rate;
- qualitative “this feels like me” score in user test.

### 11.3 User test success bar

In a small test, target users should be able to say:
- “这个角色像某种我/我身边的人”; 
- “这个句子我可能会发/存”; 
- “我知道为什么想换另一个嘴替”; 
- “没有觉得它在骂我/诊断我/教育我”.
