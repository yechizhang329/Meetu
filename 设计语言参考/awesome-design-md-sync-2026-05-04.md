- `mastercard`: useful for warm editorial cards and soft geometric/orbital layout language.
- `meta`: useful when product pages need photo-led, simple, consumer-tech clarity.

- `shopify`: useful for modern landing-page polish and accent-color control.

## Usage boundary

Treat `design-md/` as a frontend/design reference library, not a brand rulebook. For each page or asset, choose one or two relevant references and translate their design methods into Meetu's product context.

Do not directly copy another brand's palette, typography, or visual identity. Extract method: hierarchy, spacing, density, card rhythm, accent usage, image/content relationship, and responsive behavior.
