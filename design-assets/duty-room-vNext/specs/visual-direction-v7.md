## §0 v7 一屏

**画风**: 粗糙手绘嘴替动物 — 黑色 marker outline (3-4px 不规则, 比 v6 更糙) + **简化结构** (大块面, 减少细节, 禁细密眉毛/肌肉线/写实毛发) + 干净背景 (轻微画布/纸面感 OK 但不读为便签 collage) + **鲜艳色彩** + 每张必有 1 个**刺色** (高存在感小面积). 调性是"deadpan 嘴替 + 系统状态故障", 不萌不可爱不写实不 3D. 

**全图字体**: 仅允许**手写歪曲英文/数字/符号 token**: `ZZZ` / `DDL` / `404` / `1%` / `%` / `!` / `???` / `!!!`; 全部手写体, 禁电脑印刷体/monospace/terminal 字体混搭. **不进中文** (避免生图乱码, per Fiona DM 21:35).

**色彩**: 每 panel 鲜艳底 + 1 处**刺色** (荧光绿 `#1FE07B` / 电蓝 `#1854E0` / 橘红 `#E94B23` / 亮黄 `#F2EB1B` / 多巴胺粉 `#FF5C8A` / 紫粉 `#C7A4FF` 等高饱和小面积). 不冷色+黑灰主调.

**构图**: 5 panels 横排 contact sheet 2700×1080, 每 panel ~540px 宽. 干净 panel 边界 (无便签胶带/无手撕). 每 panel 自成色彩组 (底色 + 角色色 + 刺色), 5 角色不统一底色.

## §1 5 角色映射 (v7)

### A 嘴硬章鱼 (panel 1)
- **底色**: 鲜橘红 `#FF6A3D` (干净纯色)
- **主体**: 章鱼, **白色身体 + 粗糙黑 marker outline 3-4px**, **8 条腿全部绷紧硬撑** — 一些腿撑住身体下方/一些撑住地面/一些撑住画面边界; 整体姿态是 "用 8 条腿同时顶住, 不肯倒下"; **每条腿可见绷紧线条** (line tension 视觉符号, 如腿肌肉收缩/弯曲处尖角)
- **表情**: 嘴巴**紧闭一字嘴** (粗短粗黑横线, 不张口), 双眼 deadpan small dot pupils (NOT cute, NOT big), 眉毛是简短 2 笔皱起 (不细密)
- **刺色**: 荧光绿 `#1FE07B` 在腿与地面/边界接触点的 1-2 处小高光 / 或在嘴角"绷紧"位置一个小荧光绿 spike — **小面积, 强存在感**
- **NO**: 喷墨破绽 (v5 元素删) / 文字符号 / 流泪 / 流汗 / 大眼

### B 断电猫 (panel 2)
- **底色**: 鲜亮电蓝 `#3D7AE0` (干净纯色, 比 v6 灰蓝更鲜)
- **主体**: 猫, **灰白身体 + 粗糙黑 marker outline 3-4px**, **瘫成一条** (低电量姿态, 限肢摊开, 闭眼)
- **道具/符号**: 猫身上或上方**手写歪斜 `ZZZ`** (大字号, 漂浮感) + **手写歪斜 `1%`** (在猫旁的简单电池 outline 旁边)
- **刺色**: 亮黄 `#F2EB1B` 在 `ZZZ` 字 / `1%` 字 — 鲜艳手写字本身就是刺色
- **NO**: `offline` / `loading` 任何英文长词 / monospace 字体 / terminal 风 (David 21:33 #2 明示)

### C 躺平树懒 (panel 3)
- **底色**: 鲜亮草绿 `#5FB85C` (干净纯色, 鲜艳, 比 v6 暖米更年轻)
- **主体**: 树懒, **棕色身体 + 粗糙黑 marker outline 3-4px**, **sprawl 趴在 blank 草稿纸/笔记本上**, 限肢摊开, 闭眼 deadpan
- **道具/符号**: 树懒身边一根**横向进度条** (粗黑 outline 矩形, 仅 1% 填充宽度); **进度条上方手写歪斜 `DDL 1%`** (Fiona DM 21:35 推荐 — 中文 "进度 1%" 有生图乱码风险, 改用允许的英文 token "DDL" + "1%") + **手写箭头**指向进度条
- **刺色**: 橘红 `#E94B23` 在进度条 1% 填充部分 / 或 `DDL 1%` 文字 / 或手写箭头
- **NO**: 咖啡 / 课本 / 中文 (避免乱码) / 电脑印刷体

### D 整活吗喽 (panel 4)
- **底色**: 鲜亮大黄 `#FFD93D` (干净纯色, 鲜艳)
- **主体**: 猴子, **棕色身体 + 极粗糙黑 marker outline 4-5px** (D 笔触最糙), **猴自身处于抽象状态** — **简化 anatomy** (大头, 简化 muzzle, 大手大脚, 无细密眉毛/无肌肉线条/无写实毛发, 全部大块面), 身体**呆坐放空**, 一只手拿着**剥皮香蕉** (黄色香蕉皮半剥下耷拉, 白色果肉露出, 不在吃)
- **抽象状态符号**: **画幅中多个手写歪斜报错弹窗** (3-5 个, 漂浮在猴周围) — 内容是 `404` `???` `!!!` `1%` 等手写歪字 (无中文, 避免乱码); **荧光绿 `#1FE07B` + 电蓝 `#1854E0` 飞溅墨点** (小高饱和墨点 6-10 个, 散落在猴周围像系统 glitch 飞出来)
- **表情**: blank deadpan, 双眼**简单 dot pupils 或 X 字** (不画细密眉毛, 不画肌肉线), 嘴是简短弧线或小 O
- **刺色**: 荧光绿 `#1FE07B` + 电蓝 `#1854E0` 飞溅 (D 是双刺色场, 两色飞溅是 D 的视觉锚)
- **NO**: 打字机/键盘 (David 21:33 #4 明示删) / 衣服/vest/socks (v5 旧元素全删) / 反穿/失衡/不对称双眼 (v5 元素删) / 细密眉毛/肌肉线 (David 说显恐怖) / 英文长词 (`ERROR`/`offline`/`loading` 都禁)

### E 高情商刺猬 (panel 5)
- **底色**: 鲜亮多巴胺粉 `#FF5C8A` (干净纯色, 鲜艳)
- **主体**: 刺猬, **浅棕身体 + 粗糙黑 marker outline 3-4px** + **刺是清晰直立的粗黑线** (不细密, 大刺), **半蜷** (脸和一只爪子可见, 不全球)
- **道具/符号**: 刺猬周围**虚线圈** (椭圆 dashed, 表示"安全距离/别靠近")

## §2 v7 5 角色 contact sheet 主 prompt

```
A horizontal 5-panel contact sheet (2700×1080, 5 columns each ~540px
wide), in a deliberately rough hand-drawn editorial style — like a
quickly-doodled internet mood comic, NOT a polished mascot illustration.
Each panel has a different bright clean solid background color (NO
gradients, NO sticky-note paper texture, NO scotch-tape edges, NO aged
paper grain, NO collage feel — just clean flat color). Animals are drawn
with thick rough black marker outlines (3-4px irregular weight, hand-
drawn quality, NOT mechanical vector). The drawing is intentionally
SIMPLIFIED: large block-shape bodies, minimal interior detail, NO
detailed eyebrows, NO muscle lines, NO realistic fur texture, NO fine
hatching. Each panel includes ONE small-area accent "spike color" that
provides emotional punch. Hand-written wobbly characters only —
absolutely NO printed/monospace/terminal fonts. Allowed text tokens
(all in hand-written wobbly style): "ZZZ", "DDL", "404", "1%", "???",
"!!!", and short Chinese annotations of 2-4 characters at most. NO
English words other than the listed tokens. NO long sentences. NO
captions. NO logos.

PANEL 1 (vivid orange-red #FF6A3D background, clean solid color) — An
octopus with a light cream / off-white body and rough thick black marker
outline. The CRITICAL element: ALL 8 TENTACLES ARE TENSELY BRACING THE
OCTOPUS UP — some tentacles push down against the floor, some push out
against the panel's bottom edge, some prop up the body from the sides.
Visible muscle tension lines at the base of each tentacle (small angular
strain marks) showing they are all working hard. The pose reads as
"stubbornly holding it together, refusing to give an inch". The face:
mouth is a tightly compressed flat horizontal line (one short thick
black stroke), eyes are simple deadpan dot pupils (small, NOT cute, NOT
big), eyebrows are 2 simple rough strokes slightly furrowed (NOT
detailed, NOT delicate). Spike color: bright fluorescent green #1FE07B
in 1-2 small spots — at the contact points where tentacles meet
floor/edges, OR a tiny green spark at the corner of the compressed
mouth. NO text in this panel. NO ink spurt, NO tears, NO sweat — the
8-leg bracing IS the entire visual statement.

PANEL 2 (vivid electric blue #3D7AE0 background, clean solid color) — A
cat with grey-white body and rough thick black marker outline, lying
completely flat / sprawled / "powered down" — NOT curled into a cute
ball, NOT sleeping cute, but distinctly "battery has died". Limbs limp
and stretched. Face: half-closed deadpan eyes, mouth a tiny flat
horizontal line. Floating above or beside the cat: hand-written wobbly
"ZZZ" (large, drifting upward) AND a small simple battery icon outline
with hand-written wobbly "1%" beside it. Spike color: bright yellow
#F2EB1B for the "ZZZ" text and "1%" text — the wobbly hand-written text
itself is the spike. NO English words (no "offline", no "loading", no
"ESC"). NO printed monospace font. NO terminal aesthetic.

PANEL 3 (vivid grass green #5FB85C background, clean solid color) — A
sloth with a tan-brown body and rough thick black marker outline,
sprawled face-down on top of a stack of blank paper notes / notebook
pages, all four limbs spread loosely, eyes half-closed deadpan. Beside
the sloth: a horizontal progress bar drawn as a simple thick-black-
outlined rectangle, filled only ~1% on the left side. ABOVE the progress
bar, hand-written wobbly text: "DDL 1%" (just these characters, hand-
written, slightly tilted, easily readable). A small hand-drawn wobbly
arrow points from the "DDL 1%" text down to the progress bar. Spike
color: orange-red #E94B23 for the 1% fill of the progress bar AND/OR
for the "DDL 1%" hand-written text and arrow. The hand-written
annotation IS necessary — without it the progress bar reads as
decorative, not stuck.

PANEL 4 (vivid bright yellow #FFD93D background, clean solid color) — A
monkey drawn with VERY rough thick black marker outline (4-5px, the
roughest panel of the five). The monkey is intentionally SIMPLIFIED to
flat block shapes: large round head, simplified face, big simple hands
and feet, NO detailed eyebrows, NO muscle lines, NO realistic fur
texture, NO anatomical primate details — just flat block shapes with
black outline. The monkey sits in a still, zoned-out, abstract pose (NOT
active, NOT off-balance, NOT doing stunts). One hand holds a banana
that is ALREADY PEELED — yellow peel drooping in visible strips,
white banana flesh exposed, NOT being eaten. The other hand rests
casually. The monkey itself looks LOST IN AN ABSTRACT STATE. Floating
around the monkey: 3-5 hand-written wobbly error pop-up windows (small
rectangular boxes with black outline, each containing one short hand-
written wobbly text: "404", "???", "!!!", "1%" — pick a mix of
these, all hand-written tilted style). ALSO: 6-10 small splatters of
fluorescent green #1FE07B and electric blue #1854E0 ink dots scattered
around the monkey, like glitches spilling out of the system. Face:
blank deadpan, eyes are simple small dot pupils OR small "X" marks
(NOT cute, NOT big), mouth a simple short curve or tiny "O". Spike
color: fluorescent green #1FE07B PLUS electric blue #1854E0 splatters
— this is the dual-spike-color panel. NO typewriter, NO keyboard, NO
clothes, NO vest, NO socks. NO English words like "ERROR", "offline",
"loading" — only the listed wobbly tokens.

PANEL 5 (vivid dopamine pink #FF5C8A background, clean solid color) —
A hedgehog with a tan-cream body and rough thick black marker outline.
Spines are clear bold thick black lines (NOT detailed fine spines, NOT
densely-textured fur). The hedgehog is half-curled — face and one paw
visible, NOT fully balled. Around the hedgehog: a dashed oval line
traced on the ground encircling it (representing "keep distance / don't
approach"). Face: small deadpan dot eyes, mouth is a tiny compressed
polite mouth line — barely-there polite smirk, NOT warm, NOT cute, NOT
friendly, just composure holding back the spines. Spike color: light
purple #C7A4FF or bright yellow #F2EB1B for the dashed safety-distance
line OR for 1-2 spine tips. NO orange-red annotations, NO note-paper
edges, NO "?" or "!" symbols (they belong to D's panel).

OVERALL — Each panel is a self-contained rough hand-drawn editorial
illustration on its own clean solid bright background color. Panels sit
side-by-side with no visible gutters, sticky-tape edges, or paper-cut
borders — just direct color block transitions. NO sticky note collage,
NO transparent tape strips, NO aged paper texture, NO risograph
misregister, NO halftone dot grain. Background is clean (a very subtle
canvas/paper feel is acceptable but must NOT read as a sticky-note
collage). The drawing style is rough internet doodle — quick, bold,
imperfect, NOT polished, NOT mascot-IP. Hand-written tilted text only.
NO Chinese characters anywhere in the image. NO printed/monospace/
terminal fonts. NO logos, NO watermarks.
```
