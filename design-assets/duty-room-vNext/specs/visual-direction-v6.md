## §0 v6 一屏结论

**画风**: 继承社交动物测试的 **editorial weird little animals 排版小动物式** — 粗黑线 (2-3px irregular) + 扁平色块平涂 + 身体姿态带情绪 + 无大眼萌 + 无儿童可爱 + 适度怪/卡/放空. 不是 v5 的 riso/zine/手贴便签; 是 v5 之前一度 authorize 但又被拒的 editorial cartoon style, 现在重启并锁定。

**颜色**: 每个角色独立 **solid flat themeColor 底 + 白/浅底动物身体 + 黑 2-3px outline + 少量亮 accent 道具**. 5 角色各一个 theme panel, 不统一底色。**不黄滤镜** / **不暗黑 terminal** / **不 riso 错位**。

**构图**: 5 角色 contact sheet, 横排 5 panels, 每 panel solid themeColor 底 + 角色居中 + 14-18% safe padding. **卡片感清晰, 不散装**。

**画面元素 patch (David 20:12)**:
- 章鱼: 突出"嘴硬" + "**所有 8 腿都在硬撑**" (8 腿撑住身体/地面/边框, 表情嘴绷紧不承认)
- 猴子: **手拿剥开的香蕉** + **身体呆坐放空** (不过度动态整活) + **脑中/头顶显示 `404`** (脑断线/系统找不到)

## §1 5 角色映射 (v6 排版小动物式)

参照社交动物测试 "weird editorial personality mascots, flat graphic line art, imperfect proportions, deadpan eyes" 调性。每角色: 动物自身 + 1-2 件 blank 或简单符号道具 + 姿势/表情承担人格表达。

### A 嘴硬章鱼 (主 patch per David)
- **theme 底色**: 橘红 `#E94B23` (张力色, 贴 A "嘴硬"气)
- **主体**: 章鱼, 白色身体 + 黑粗线 outline, **所有 8 腿都在硬撑 (撑住身体 / 撑地面 / 撑画面下沿)**; 姿势是 "八腿绷紧, 像在用力顶住什么"
- **表情**: 嘴巴紧闭成"一字线"或小 U 绷紧线, 眉毛皱起来但眼睛是 deadpan dot pupils, 整体"不承认但嘴角绷住"
- **accent 道具**: 不需要别的道具; 8 腿绷紧 + 嘴紧闭已足够
- **雷点**: 不要给章鱼画"软绵绵 cute"造型; 不要喷墨; 不要大眼

### B 断电猫
- **theme 底色**: 灰蓝 `#5D7186` (省电猫 themeColor 沿用)
- **主体**: 猫, 灰白身体 + 黑 outline, **瘫成一条/低电量姿态**; 旁边身上有一个电池格符号 (半空, 2-3 格)
- **表情**: 半闭眼 deadpan, 嘴是小弧线/平线
- **accent**: 浅蓝 `#9DD7FF` 局部 (电池格 / 电量低符号, 小面积)
- **雷点**: 不做萌系 cute, 不做 ball 蜷 (那是 v5 风格); 社交动物测试 style 是"瘫断电"不是"睡觉球"

### C 躺平树懒
- **theme 底色**: 暖米 `#D7BD82` (贴 C 便签/旧纸感)
- **主体**: 树懒, 浅棕身体 + 黑 outline, **sprawl 趴在一叠 blank 草稿纸/SOP 卡上**, 半闭眼 deadpan
- **道具/符号**: 身下或旁边一张 blank 进度条 (10% 填充, 其他空白), 或一个 blank 文件夹开着
- **accent**: 深棕 `#7A5A3E` outline 强化 + blank 进度条的 black outline
- **雷点**: 不做可爱睡觉姿态 (眼睛闭着但不是 ball); 没有 "咖啡/课本" 细节 (v5 元素删除, 简化到 blank 道具); 不做橘红批注感 (那是 E)

### D 整活吗喽 (主 patch per David)
- **theme 底色**: 鲜黄 `#FFE15A` (贴 D 整活明亮)
- **主体**: 猴子, 棕色身体 + 黑 outline, **身体呆坐放空 (不过度动态)**, 一只手拿着**一只已剥开的香蕉** (香蕉皮半剥下耷拉, 果肉露出, 猴不在吃), 另一只手垂下/放膝盖上
- **脑/头顶**: **头顶 / 脑袋上方显示 `404`** 符号 (字号明显, 像 system error pop-up)
- **表情**: **呆/放空 deadpan**, 双眼简单 dot pupils 或 X 眼, 嘴是简短平线或小 O; 不再是"asymmetric 不对称双眼" (v5 元素简化删除)
- **accent**: 橘红 `#E94B23` 或 terminal 绿 `#1FE07B` 在 `404` 文字或小符号, 小面积
- **服饰**: **不穿衣服** (v5 的反穿 vest + mismatched 袜 全部删除, David 简化)
- **雷点**: 不要 v5 的失衡姿态 / 键盘飞键帽 / 反穿 / mismatched — 全部删; 只保留: 呆坐 + 剥皮香蕉 + 脑中 404

### E 高情商刺猬
- **theme 底色**: 棕灰 `#9B8068` (贴 E 边界刺猬 themeColor)
- **主体**: 刺猬, 浅棕身体 + 黑 outline + 刺是 clear 直立黑线 spikes; **半蜷但露出脸**, 周围有**虚线圈** (暗示"别靠近")
- **表情**: 眼神小 dot + 嘴唇是 "tiny compressed polite mouth line" (barely-there polite smirk, 不治愈不 warm 不 cute)
- **道具/符号**: **不需要橘红批注** (v5 元素删除); 社交动物测试 style 是 "周围有安全距离虚线 + 刺像小路障"
- **accent**: 紫色 `#C7A4FF` 或橘红 `#E94B23` 在虚线圈 outline, 小面积
- **雷点**: 不做治愈萌宠; 刺必须明显; 半蜷不全蜷

## §2 v6 5 角色 contact sheet 主 prompt

```
A horizontal 5-panel contact sheet (2700×1080, 5 columns each ~540px
wide), in the visual style of "social animal test" — editorial weird
little animals, flat graphic line art with rough black outlines (2-3px
irregular, hand-drawn feel, NEVER pure mechanical vector), deadpan
personality mascots, imperfect proportions, absurd internet doodle
energy but still clear and readable. Each panel has a different flat
solid themeColor background (NO gradients, NO textures, NO paper grain,
NO risograph misregister, NO halftone). Animals are mostly white/light
body with bold black outline; small accent color pops in one or two
spots per panel. NOT cute pet stickers, NOT children's storybook, NOT
kawaii, NOT Sanrio sweet, NOT chibi, NOT mascot IP polish, NOT realistic
anatomy, NOT 3D rendered. Deadpan anxious internet doodles, offbeat
college meme characters, weird but readable.

PANEL 1 (orange-red #E94B23 solid background) — An octopus drawn in
rough flat black line work with a light-cream/off-white body. The
octopus MUST visibly use all 8 tentacles to brace/hold itself up — all
8 legs are tensely pushing against the ground / the bottom of the panel
frame / or bracing the body from all sides. The posture reads as
"stubbornly holding it together, not giving an inch". The face: mouth
tightly pressed into a compressed flat line or small tense U-shape,
eyebrows slightly furrowed, eyes are simple deadpan dot pupils (NOT big,
NOT cute). No tears, no ink spurt, no sweat — the 8-leg bracing IS the
visual statement. NO text in this panel.

PANEL 2 (slate-blue #5D7186 solid background) — A cat drawn in flat
black line work with grey-white body, lying completely flat / slumped /
"low battery" posture with limbs stretched out limp. On the cat's body
or beside it: a small battery-level icon with 2-3 grid segments filled
(light blue #9DD7FF small accent). Face: half-closed deadpan eyes,
mouth a tiny flat line or slight arc. The cat looks "powered down", NOT
sleeping in a cute ball. NO "ZZZ" text, NO "1%" text (the low-battery
icon alone conveys the state).

PANEL 3 (warm tan #D7BD82 solid background) — A sloth drawn in flat
black line work with a light body, sprawled face-down on top of a stack
of blank paper notes or draft sheets, limbs spread out loosely, eyes
half-closed deadpan. Beside the sloth: a blank horizontal progress bar
showing only ~10-15% filled, and/or an open blank folder sketched in
line-art. NO text on the notes or progress bar — all surfaces are blank.

PANEL 4 (bright yellow #FFE15A solid background) — A monkey drawn in
flat black line work with brown body, SITTING IN A PLAIN STILL POSE
(deadpan and zoned-out, NOT active, NOT off-balance, NOT doing stunts).
One hand holds a banana that is ALREADY PEELED — the yellow peel droops
downward in visible strips, and the white banana flesh is exposed. The
monkey is NOT eating the banana, just holding it. The other hand rests
casually on the knee or lap. Above the monkey's head, floating in the
air like a pop-up error message: the text "404" in a blocky sans-serif
style (small accent color #E94B23 orange-red or #1FE07B terminal-green
is fine for the "404" glyph). Face: blank deadpan, eyes simple dot
pupils or "X" marks, mouth a simple flat line or tiny "O". NO clothes,
NO vest, NO socks, NO keyboard — only the peeled banana and the "404"
thought bubble. The total reading must be "brain has disconnected,
system not found".

PANEL 5 (warm brown #9B8068 solid background) — A hedgehog drawn in
flat black line work with a light body, half-curled (face and one paw
visible, NOT fully balled), spines extending outward as clear bold
black lines — NOT bared aggressively but distinctly present. Around
the hedgehog: a dashed oval "keep distance" line traced on the ground
around it (small accent color #C7A4FF purple or #E94B23 orange-red for
the dashed line). Face: small deadpan eye dots, mouth a tiny
compressed polite mouth line (barely-there polite smirk, NOT warm,
NOT cute, NOT friendly — composure holding back the spines). NO
annotation marks, NO "?"/"!" symbols, NO note-paper edges. The safety
distance dashed line IS the visual signature.

OVERALL — Each panel is a self-contained editorial-style flat
illustration on its own solid themeColor background. Panels sit side by
side with no visible gutters or borders between them — just the direct
color block change. Animals do not cross panel boundaries. NO text
anywhere in the image EXCEPT the "404" in panel 4. NO Chinese
characters. NO captions, labels, logos, or watermarks. NO yellow
filter over the whole image. NO risograph misregister. NO halftone. NO
aged paper texture. NO riso/zine aesthetic. The style should feel like
a young adult internet doodle comic, NOT a branded mascot IP, NOT a
polished advertising illustration.
```
